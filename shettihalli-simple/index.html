Our Story

Where Every Mango
Carries a Memory

In 1962, our grandfather planted the first mango sapling on a sloping acre of red laterite land in Shettihalli, Hassan District, Karnataka. One tree became a hundred — but our farming philosophy never changed.

No synthetic chemicals. No shortcuts. No compromises. Cow-dung compost, neem-based pest control, and patient waiting for nature's perfect timing.

"Our mangoes don't just feed your body — they connect you to the earth, to the seasons, and to a way of life worth preserving."
— The Shettihalli Family
